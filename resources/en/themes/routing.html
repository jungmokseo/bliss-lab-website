Stretchable conductors and interconnects

Wearable and soft electronics need to carry signals and power as they move. This theme explores electrical pathways made from liquid metals, conductive fibers and hydrogels, and the relationship between deformation and electrical behavior. Sensing electrodes and interconnects are discussed according to their different purposes.
