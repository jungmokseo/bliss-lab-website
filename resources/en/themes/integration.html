Device integration and packaging

Connecting different materials and components involves more than electrical contact. A junction may also need to tolerate deformation, provide insulation and protect the device. This theme covers chip-to-soft-circuit connections, sensor-system assembly and encapsulation, alongside related materials and device-physics research.
