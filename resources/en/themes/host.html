Biological responses and surface design

Proteins, bacteria and tissue can interact with medical-device and biomaterial surfaces in different ways. This theme examines materials designed to reduce unwanted attachment, friction or inflammation while supporting useful biological interactions. Summaries distinguish laboratory models, animal studies and clinical evidence.
