Tissue–biomaterial interface. Classification centers on tissue contact, mechanical adaptation and analyte transport.

Materials in contact with tissue

A tissue-contacting material needs more than adhesion. It may also need to move with the body and allow selected molecules to pass through. This theme brings together hydrogel and bioadhesive design, tissue repair and studies of cell–material interactions.
