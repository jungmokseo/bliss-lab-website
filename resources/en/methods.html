About this library

This library brings together BLISS Lab publications, research summaries and related news.

Papers and summaries

Each paper page provides bibliographic details, a DOI where available, and an introduction to the research question and approach. The level of detail depends on the sources available. Summaries based on an abstract are identified as such; summaries drawing on the main text state which material was used. These pages are research summaries prepared for the BLISS Lab website. The listed authors and publication details identify the original paper; the summary text is editorial commentary, not the original paper or its author-written abstract. Use the DOI link to consult or cite the original paper.

Browse by research theme

Papers are grouped into four themes: materials in contact with tissue, stretchable conductors and interconnects, device integration and packaging, and biological responses and surface design. Related themes are linked where useful. These groupings support browsing; not every paper directly builds or evaluates a bioelectronic device.

Source coverage

Where only a publication listing is available, the page provides bibliographic information. Summaries based on an abstract or selected sections stay within those sources. Reading the main text does not mean that all supplementary materials and underlying data have been reviewed. See ‘Sources used in this summary’ on each paper page for details.

Reading results in context

Findings should be read alongside the materials, controls and test conditions used in the study. We do not rank performance across papers with different test conditions or describe cell and animal results as evidence of clinical benefit. Questions for further study are distinguished from established findings.

Publication details

Titles, authors, journal information and DOIs are drawn from publisher records and public bibliographic sources. Online publication dates may differ from the year assigned to a journal issue. Entries follow the publication details and identify papers whose final publication information is not yet confirmed.

Figures and full papers

Links direct readers to publisher pages or public repositories. Reproduced figures include their source, figure number and reuse terms. AI-generated concept illustrations are labeled and kept distinct from experimental findings. Public access to an article does not by itself establish permission to reuse its figures.

Research news

University announcements, news reports and researcher posts offer additional ways to learn about the work. Coverage is distinguished from the findings in the paper, and articles based on the same announcement are not treated as separate experimental evidence.
