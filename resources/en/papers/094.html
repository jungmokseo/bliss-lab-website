Intelligent multimodal-energy-driven piezoelectric antibacterial platforms: From structural control to system-level diagnosis

Jiaqi Liu†, Jun Li†, Huiyan Sun, Jungmok Seo, Yanmei Zhang*, Yunlong Yu*

Materials Today Bio, 38, 103299 (2026)

DOI: 10.1016/j.mtbio.2026.103299 ↗

Why this research matters

This review examines the structural and system design of piezoelectric antibacterial platforms driven by multiple energy sources. Its abstract connects charge separation, external stimulation and AI with wound, implant and localized-infection applications, while highlighting stability and biosafety challenges.

What the study did

This review examines piezoelectric antibacterial platforms that combine multiple energy inputs and artificial intelligence.

It connects material structure, charge separation and external stimulation with applications in wounds, implants and localized infections.

What remains to be answered

The authors identify stability, biosafety and scale-up as open challenges. The review does not establish clinical usefulness of AI.

What to compare

Useful comparisons require matched pathogens, energy input and exposure, anatomical site, controls and host-toxicity assessment.

Related research

Heterostructured piezocatalytic nanoparticles with enhanced ultrasound response for efficient repair of infectious bone defects
