pH-Responsive DNA Nanolinker Conjugated Hybrid Materials for Electrochemical Microactuator and Biosensor Applications

Yasamin A. Jodat, Parisa Lotfi, Parisa Pour Shahid Saeed Abadi, Ji-Young Mun, Jungmok Seo, Eun Ae Shin, Sung Mi Jung, Chang Kee Lee*, Su Ryon Shin*

ACS Applied Nano Materials, 1(12), 6630-6640, (2018)

Jungmok Seo, who now leads the BLISS Lab in the School of Electrical and Electronic Engineering at Yonsei University, is a co-author of this paper.

DOI: 10.1021/acsanm.8b01429 ↗

The question behind this study

DNA nanolinkers connect carbon nanotubes into microfibers whose structure and electrochemical response change with pH. The study explores these hybrid fibers for electrochemical actuation and hydrogen-peroxide sensing.

What the study shows

i-motif DNA links a single-walled carbon-nanotube (SWCNT) network, changing the hybrid microfiber’s structural and electrochemical response to pH.

The conclusion and experimental section describe wet spinning, pH response, electrochemical actuation and hydrogen-peroxide sensing.
