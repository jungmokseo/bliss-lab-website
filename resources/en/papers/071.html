Functionalized EGaIn Electrodes with Tunable Reduced‐Graphene‐Oxide Assembled EGaIn Core–Shell Particles for Soft and Deformable Electrochemical Biosensors

Dongwook Lee, Sunghyun Park, Jungmok Seo, Won-Yong Lee, Min-gu Kim*, Jayoung Kim*

Advanced Functional Materials, 34(31), 2311696 (2024)

DOI: 10.1002/adfm.202311696 ↗

Research context and placement

Surface-functionalized EGaIn particles form deformable electrochemical electrodes. The work connects to liquid conductors, but its focus on electrode reactivity rather than interconnect continuity makes this an extended assignment.

Boundary case: assigned to the closest interface for navigation. Read the rationale and cross-links together; the primary theme does not delimit the study. Placement of foundational work does not establish direct bioelectronic application.

Research overview and key contributions

rGO/Pt/Au-functionalized EGaIn particles adhere to a liquid-metal collector; buffer-based sensing and post-stretch electrochemical responses were evaluated.

Functional surfaces address oxide-limited charge transfer. The experiments include CV preconditioning to stabilize the electrode.

Open challenges

The reviewed buffer tests do not establish long-term in-vivo performance; biofluid interference, preconditioning and longevity require further checks.

Comparison context

Match preconditioning, analyte/electrolyte/reference, active area, metal/enzyme functionalization and deformation history.
