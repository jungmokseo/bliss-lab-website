PUBLICATION 041 / 2018Summary based on the main text

High-throughput approaches for screening and analysis of cell behaviors

Jungmok Seo, Jung-Youn Shin, Jeroen Leijten, Oju Jeon, Gulden Camci-Unal, Anna D. Dikina, Katelyn Brinegar, Amir M. Ghaemmaghami, Eben Alsberg*, Ali Khademhosseini*

Biomaterials, 153, 85-101 (2018)

DOI: 10.1016/j.biomaterials.2017.06.022 ↗

The question behind this study

This review examines high-throughput methods for comparing how cells respond to different culture conditions. It connects cell–material environments, biomolecule delivery and analysis of secreted products with the choice of cellular readouts.

What the study shows

The review connects high-throughput cell–material environments, biomolecule delivery and secreted-product analysis.

It provides a framework linking combinatorial culture conditions to response measurement.

Open questions

The review identifies validation across multiple conditions and better links between culture and analysis platforms as open needs.

Conditions for comparison

Distinguish two- and three-dimensional culture, cell sources, biomolecules, readouts and actual analysis time.
