A Remotely Actuated Multifunctional Nitinol–PMMA Smart Biocomposite: Microcellular Foaming, Shape Morphing, and Controlled Drug Release

Donghwan Lim, Jaehoo Kim, Tae Young Kim, Jiwon Kwon, Jaewoo Kim, Jungmok Seo, Yong Hoon Jang, Sung Woon Cha, and Jun Young Yoon*

ACS Applied Materials & Interfaces, 17(51), 70047 - 70058 (2025)

DOI: 10.1021/acsami.5c19771 ↗

Why this research matters

A Nitinol–PMMA composite combines induction-heated shape recovery, microcellular foaming and release of a model agent. Quantified release was measured in buffer after specimens were prefoamed on a hot plate, not through remotely triggered drug release inside the body.

What the study did

The Nitinol–PMMA composite combines induction-heated shape recovery, microcellular foaming and release of a model substance.

The selected text reports weaker interfacial bonding after foaming and a need for better shape control. Quantitative NaBz-release tests used specimens prefoamed on a 60°C hot plate, then placed in PBS.

What remains to be answered

NIH-3T3 cell compatibility and in-vitro demonstrations do not establish animal or human treatment effects. The full Results section, supplementary information, long-term interface stability and remotely triggered release inside the body remain unreviewed or unverified.

What to compare

Compare specimen geometry, heating history, porosity, payload, release medium and cell-assay conditions.
