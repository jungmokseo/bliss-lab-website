Nanomaterials on flexible substrates to explore innovative functions: From energy harvesting to bio-integrated electronics

Ja Hoon Koo, Jungmok Seo, Taeyoon Lee*

Thin Solid Films, 524, 1-19 (2012)

Jungmok Seo, who now leads the BLISS Lab in the School of Electrical and Electronic Engineering at Yonsei University, is a co-author of this paper.

DOI: 10.1016/j.tsf.2012.10.001 ↗

Background

This 2012 review surveys nanomaterials on flexible substrates for energy harvesting, sensors and biointegrated circuits, connecting materials and fabrication methods with mechanics and device functions.

What the review covers

This 2012 review connects nanomaterials on flexible substrates with energy harvesting, sensors and biointegrated circuits.

It brings together the materials, fabrication methods, mechanics and device functions reported at that time.

Questions and limits

The challenges include uniform large-area fabrication, performance during deformation, and fatigue associated with residual stress. Read the review as a view of the field in 2012.

How to compare the results

For each example, consult the original study’s materials, deformation conditions and biological model before making comparisons.
