Research context and placement

The abstract supports coated conductive fibres and woven pressure sensing. Keep the conductive-fibre shelf, flagged because transduction does not establish strain-invariant routing.

Research overview and key contributions

Dielectric-rubber-coated conductive fibres form a flexible textile pressure sensor for woven glove/clothing wireless controls.

Conductive fibres become sensing elements within weavable structures; abstract superiority claims were not independently audited.
