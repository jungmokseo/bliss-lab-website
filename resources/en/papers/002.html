Trade-Off Relationship of Size and Density of Platinum Nanocrystal in Nonvolatile Memory Characteristics

Jungmok Seo, Taeyoon Lee*

Japanese Journal of Applied Physics, 49(10R), 104003 (2010)

DOI: 10.1143/jjap.49.104003 ↗

Background

This study examines how the size and density of platinum nanocrystals affect the trade-off between storing more charge and retaining it for longer in memory devices.

What the study found

The study examines how platinum nanocrystal size and density affect charge storage and retention in metal–oxide–semiconductor (MOS) memory.

It explains changes in storage through the electric field across the tunnel oxide and the probability of electrons tunnelling through it.

Questions and limits

A wider storage window does not establish longer charge retention. The endurance of a complete memory array also needs separate testing.

How to compare the results

Compare nanocrystal size and density distributions, oxide thickness, programming voltage, and how retention time is defined.
