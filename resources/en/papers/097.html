Immune‐Responsive Bioinks Enable Vision‐Guided in Situ Robotic Bioprinting to Accelerate Diabetic Wound Healing

Seol-Ha Jeong†, Eleftheria-Angeliki Valsami†, Ki-Tae Kim†, Kijun Park†, Jihyun Kim, Javier Alejandro Lozano Soto, Jaeseo Lee, Rafael Alejandro Cornejo Rodríguez, Danilo Martins dos Santos, Jarno Hiemstra, Mei L. L. Cham-Pérez, Montserrat Legorreta Gonzalez, Soo A Kim, Jungmok Seo, Zhuqing Li, Brendan Craig Thibault, Nebras Sobahi, Yejin Jo, Mohammad Asif Hussain, Kyungsang Kim, Lance Fiondella, Aristidis Veves, Woo-Jin Kim*, Georgios Theocharidis*, Su Ryon Shin*

Advanced Functional Materials, 36(67), e77191 (2026)

DOI: 10.1002/adfm.77191 ↗

Why this research matters

A vision-guided robot prints bioink containing oxygen-generating zein microparticles and human mesenchymal stem cells directly into wounds. The abstract describes hypoxia and immune modulation; the use of human cells does not indicate a clinical trial.

What the study did

A vision-guided robot prints bioink containing oxygen-generating zein microparticles and human mesenchymal stem cells into wounds.

The approach combines shape-adapted printing with changes to hypoxia and the immune environment. Macrophage associations in the abstract do not isolate each component's causal contribution.

What remains to be answered

Which controls separate the effects of oxygen, cells and printing, and what evidence establishes longer-term safety? These questions require the main text and supporting data.

What to compare

Comparisons need matched diabetes models, wound size, oxygen release, cell dose, controls, immune-assessment timing and printing conformity.

Related research

In Situ 3D Bioprinting Living Photosynthetic Scaffolds for Autotrophic Wound Healing

DOI: 10.34133/2022/9794745 ↗

The PubMed abstract describes wound printing with microalgae that produce oxygen under light. It offers a comparison with the oxygen-generating particles and stem cells used here.

Light requirements, biological components and models differ. The two abstracts cannot establish superior immune regulation or wound closure.
