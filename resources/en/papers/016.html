Research overview and key contributions

SWNT–polymer interfaces aid X-ray-induced charge separation, but greater loading also increases dark current and slows response.

The study couples sensitivity gains to loading-dependent signal-to-noise and temporal-stability trade-offs.

Open challenges

Editorial question: do irradiation history, dose rate and bending shift the optimum loading?
