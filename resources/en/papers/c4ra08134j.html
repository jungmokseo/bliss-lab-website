Coupled self-assembled monolayer for enhancement of Cu diffusion barrier and adhesion properties

Yongwon Chung, Sanggeun Lee, Chandreswar Mahata, Jungmok Seo, Seungmin Lim, Minsu Jeong, Hanearl Jung, Young-chang Joo, Young-bae Park, Hyungjun Kim, Taeyoon Lee*

RSC Advances, 4(104), 60123-60130 (2014)

Jungmok Seo, who now leads the BLISS Lab in the School of Electrical and Electronic Engineering at Yonsei University, is a co-author of this paper.

DOI: 10.1039/c4ra08134j ↗

Background

Chemically coupled molecular layers between copper and silicon dioxide are designed to limit copper diffusion and strengthen adhesion. Diffusion-barrier and bending-based adhesion tests evaluate these two functions.

What the study found

Coupling APTMS and MPA monolayers creates an interface designed to limit copper diffusion and improve adhesion between silicon dioxide and copper.

Diffusion-failure tests and bending-based adhesion tests assess the interface’s barrier and bonding functions together.

Questions and limits

The results concern a semiconductor stack. Stretchable wiring and protection from biological fluids require separate validation.

How to compare the results

Compare barrier thickness and coverage, electric field, temperature, stack structure, and where fracture occurs.
