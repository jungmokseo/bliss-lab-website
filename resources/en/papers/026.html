Proton irradiation energy dependence of defect formation in graphene

Sanggeun Lee, Jungmok Seo, Juree Hong, Seul Hyun Park, Joo-Hee Lee, Byung-Wook Min, Taeyoon Lee*

Applied Surface Science, 344, 52-56 (2015)

DOI: 10.1016/j.apsusc.2015.03.107 ↗

Background

This study asks how proton energy changes defects and electrical behavior in graphene at a fixed irradiation fluence. It combines Raman spectroscopy and transistor measurements with energy-transfer calculations.

What the study found

At a fixed proton fluence, changing proton energy produces different graphene defect signals and transistor responses.

The study connects defect types identified by Raman spectroscopy and electrical measurements with energy-transfer calculations.

Questions and limits

The precise origin of the flattening near the Dirac region at high energy remains unresolved in the paper.

How to compare the results

Compare proton energy and fluence, silicon-dioxide support, graphene layer count, time after exposure, and Raman normalization.
