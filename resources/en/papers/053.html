Flexible and Stretchable PEDOT‐Embedded Hybrid Substrates for Bioengineering and Sensory Applications

Afsoon Fallahi, Serena Mandla, Thomas Kerr-Phillip, Jungmok Seo, Raquel O. Rodrigues, Yasamin A. Jodat, Roya Samanipour, Mohammad Asif Hussain, Chang Kee Lee, Hojae Bae, Ali Khademhosseini*, Jadranka Travas-Sejdic*, and Su Ryon Shin*

ChemNanoMat, 5(6), 729-737 (2019)

Jungmok Seo, who now leads the BLISS Lab in the School of Electrical and Electronic Engineering at Yonsei University, is a co-author of this paper.

DOI: 10.1002/cnma.201900146 ↗

The question behind this study

Embedding the conductive polymer PEDOT in electrospun fiber mats combines strain-responsive electrical behavior with support for cultured cardiac cells. The study examines how fiber alignment changes mechanical and electrical directionality.

What the study shows

PEDOT embedded in NBR/PEGDM electrospun mats combines strain-responsive conduction with cardiac-cell support.

Fiber alignment tunes mechanical/electrical anisotropy, but did not directly produce the expected cell alignment.

Open questions

Conditions for comparison

Comparisons need matched fiber directions, PEDOT contents, strain histories, cell types and culture durations.
