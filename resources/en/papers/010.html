Reversible wettability control of silicon nanowire surfaces: From superhydrophilicity to superhydrophobicity

Jungmok Seo, Soonil Lee, Heetak Han, Youngwon Chung, Jaehong Lee, Sung-Dae Kim, Young-Woon Kim, Sangwoo Lim, Taeyoon Lee*

Thin Solid Films, 527, 179-185 (2013)

DOI: 10.1016/j.tsf.2012.12.010 ↗

Research context and placement

Water–nanowire wetting is a surface foundation without verified protein fouling or immune response.

Boundary case: assigned to the closest interface for navigation. Read the rationale and cross-links together; the primary theme does not delimit the study. Placement of foundational work does not establish direct bioelectronic application.

Research overview and key contributions

Cyclic oxygen annealing creates water-adherent superhydrophobic silicon nanowires.

Silanol/siloxane changes and rewetting in air explain wettability resetting.
