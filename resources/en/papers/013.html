Research overview and key contributions

Water capillarity transfers silver nanoparticle lines from wrinkle templates onto hydrophilic substrates.

It avoids added glue and evaluates SERS with probe molecules.

Silver nanoparticles assemble on a buckled template and transfer to a flat substrate through water-mediated capillary forces without glue. The original image is not reproduced because reusable figure rights were not established. A source link is provided.
