Effects of H2 ambient annealing in fully 002-textured ZnO:Ga thin films grown on glass substrates using RF magnetron co-sputter deposition

Sungyeon Kim, Jungmok Seo, Hyeon Woo Jang, Jungsik Bang, Woong Lee, Taeyoon Lee, Jae-Min Myoung*

Applied Surface Science, 255 (8), 4616-4622 (2009)

DOI: 10.1016/j.apsusc.2008.11.085 ↗

Background

This study examines how heating gallium-doped zinc oxide films in hydrogen changes their electrical and optical properties for use as transparent electrodes on glass.

What the study found

The study uses hydrogen annealing to change the electrical and optical properties of gallium-doped zinc oxide films on glass. It connects these changes to oxide reduction and dopant activation.

The results inform processing choices for improving the conductivity of co-sputtered transparent electrodes.

Questions and limits

The study does not establish durability under repeated bending on flexible substrates or compatibility with biological contact.

How to compare the results

To compare annealing effects, account for gallium content, deposition and annealing conditions, film thickness, and the reference used for optical transmission.
