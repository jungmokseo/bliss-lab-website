Highly Sensitive Multifilament Fiber Strain Sensors with Ultrabroad Sensing Range for Textile Electronics

Jaehong Lee, Sera Shin, Sanggeun Lee, Jaekang Song, Subin Kang, Heetak Han, SeulGee Kim, Seunghoe Kim, Jungmok Seo, DaeEun Kim, Taeyoon Lee*

ACS Nano, 12(5), 4259-4268 (2018)

DOI: 10.1021/acsnano.7b07795 ↗

The question behind this study

A conductive multifilament fiber incorporating silver nanoparticles senses strain through changes in resistance as it stretches. Sensitivity and sensing range are evaluated separately for the first stretch and subsequent loading.

What the study shows

Ag nanoparticles and Ag-rich multifilament fibers provide a deformable strain-sensing structure.

Sensitivity and range are reported separately for first and subsequent stretching.

Open questions

Preconditioning and long-term signal drift need consistent evaluation before repeated-use performance can be compared.

Conditions for comparison

Separate first and repeated stretching, and match strain ranges, baseline resistance and fatigue-test conditions.
