Research context and placement

Public text directly examines the device–host boundary through insertion friction, attachment and electrode impedance.

Research overview and key contributions

LIPS targets insertion friction and nonspecific adhesion through a lubricated neural-probe interface.

Increased Pt-black area offsets the impedance penalty of lubrication.

Open challenges

Editorial question: connect lubricant retention with repeated micromotion and longer implantation periods.

Comparison context

Editorial comparison: match electrode roughness, lubricant, insertion speed and recording criteria to separate surface and electrical effects.
