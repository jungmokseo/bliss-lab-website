Volume, issue, pages or article-number notation was reconciled with public publication metadata checked on 3 October 2026. This bibliographic check is separate from main-text review.

Research context and placement

A liquid interface addresses blood/mucus fouling and fogging on an endoscope cap, fitting antifouling during host contact.

Research overview and key contributions

This study addresses fogging and blood/mucus fouling on disposable endoscope caps. SHIELD-C is a disposable endoscope cap combining a PDMS brush with silicone oil.

Its transparent liquid interface and lubricant replenishment address blood and mucus fouling.
