Jungmok Seo, Jungyoun Shin, Jeroen Leijten, Oju Jeon, Ayça Bal Öztürk, Jeroen Rouwkema, Yuancheng Li, Su Ryon Shin, Hadi Hajiali, Eben Alsberg*, Ali Khademhosseini*

DOI: 10.1021/acsami.7b17991 ↗

Research context and placement

Cell–matrix mechanics is foundational; 3D culture compression does not establish tissue adhesion or implanted-device host response.

Research overview and key contributions

Connectable compression modules screen GelMA concentration and cyclic loading effects on 3D MSC cultures.

The system enables parallel evaluation of matrix variables and dynamic mechanical stimulation.

Open challenges

Editorial question: how independently can module strain and matrix-concentration effects be resolved?
