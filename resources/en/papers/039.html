PUBLICATION 039 / 2017Summary based on the main text

Electroconductive nanoscale topography for enhanced neuronal differentiation and electrophysiological maturation of human neural stem cells

Kisuk Yang, Seung Jung Yu, Jong Seung Lee, Hak-Rae Lee, Gyeong-Eon Chang, Jungmok Seo, Taeyoon Lee, Eunji Cheong*, Sung Gap Im*, Seung-Woo Cho*

Nanoscale, 9(47), 18737-18752 (2017)

DOI: 10.1039/c7nr05446g ↗

The question behind this study

Human neural stem cells were cultured on titanium-coated nanogratings to study alignment, differentiation and electrophysiological maturation. Coated flat surfaces and uncoated gratings helped examine the combined effects of conductivity and surface topography.

What the study shows

Titanium-coated nanogratings were evaluated for neural stem-cell alignment, differentiation and electrophysiological maturation.

Coated-flat and uncoated-grating controls probe combined conductivity and topography effects.

Comparisons need matched grating geometry, coatings, culture duration, electrical pulses and measures of cell maturity.
