Hybrid functional microfibers for textile electronics and biosensors

Bichitra Nanda Sahoo, Byungwoo Choi, Jungmok Seo, Taeyoon Lee*

Journal of Semiconductors, 39 (1), 011009 (2018)

DOI: 10.1088/1674-4926/39/1/011009 ↗

The question behind this study

This review explores how carbon and metal nanomaterials and hydrogels give microfibers multiple functions. Applications span textile sensors, biosensing, drug delivery and tissue fabrication.

What the study shows

The review covers multifunctional fibers incorporating carbon/metal nanomaterials and hydrogels.

It links textile sensing, biosensing, drug delivery and tissue fabrication through fiber functionalization.
