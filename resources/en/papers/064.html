Giant Pressure Output Efficiency of Capacitive Micromachined Ultrasonic Transducers Using Nano-Silicon-Springs

Hae Youn Kim†, Dong-Hyun Kang†, Shyinyong Shim, Jungmok Seo, Butrus T. Khuri-Yakub, Byung Chul Lee*

2022 IEEE International Ultrasonics Symposium (IUS), 1 - 4 (2022)

DOI: 10.1109/ius54386.2022.9958066 ↗

The question behind this study

This conference paper investigates nano-silicon springs in a capacitive micromachined ultrasonic transducer to increase plate displacement and acoustic output efficiency. It covers device fabrication and vibration/pressure testing, rather than a validated wearable or clinical imaging system.

What the study shows

This conference paper uses nano-silicon springs in a capacitive micromachined ultrasonic transducer (CMUT) to increase average piston-plate displacement and acoustic output efficiency.

It describes SOI-wafer fabrication and impedance, laser-vibrometry and hydrophone tests. Pressure measured at a distance in oil differs from a calculated surface maximum, just as DC bias differs from AC pulse amplitude.
