The effects of surface modification on the electrical properties of p–n + junction silicon nanowires grown by an aqueous electroless etching method

Seulah Lee, Ja Hoon Koo, Jungmok Seo, Sung-Dae Kim, Kwang Hyun Lee, Seongil Im, Young-Woon Kim, Taeyoon Lee*

Journal of Nanoparticle Research, 14(5), 840 (2012)

Jungmok Seo, who now leads the BLISS Lab in the School of Electrical and Electronic Engineering at Yonsei University, is a co-author of this paper.

DOI: 10.1007/s11051-012-0840-6 ↗

Background

Oxidation followed by oxide removal smooths silicon nanowires and changes their electrical and light-response behavior. Improved forward and leakage-current characteristics come with reduced photocurrent.

What the study found

Oxidation followed by oxide removal smooths silicon nanowires and changes the current through their junctions.

The study links improvements in forward-current and leakage behaviour to reduced surface roughness and defects.

Questions and limits

Photocurrent decreases after smoothing. The electrical improvements therefore do not establish an improvement in every aspect of photodetection.

How to compare the results

Compare wire diameter, absorbing volume, illumination, junction area, and bias voltage.

Related reading

The related review’s abstract describes how metals, etchants and substrates affect etching. It helps separate the initial fabrication conditions from the post-treatment studied here.

The review does not independently validate the charge-transport mechanism proposed for this treatment.
