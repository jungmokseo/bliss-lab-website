The effects of surface modification on the electrical properties of p–n + junction silicon nanowires grown by an aqueous electroless etching method

Seulah Lee, Ja Hoon Koo, Jungmok Seo, Sung-Dae Kim, Kwang Hyun Lee, Seongil Im, Young-Woon Kim, Taeyoon Lee*

Journal of Nanoparticle Research, 14, 840-1 (2012)

DOI: 10.1007/s11051-012-0840-6 ↗

Research context and placement

Surface smoothing and junction optoelectronics support semiconductor foundations rather than biological interfaces.

Boundary case: assigned to the closest interface for navigation. Read the rationale and cross-links together; the primary theme does not delimit the study. Placement of foundational work does not establish direct bioelectronic application.

Research overview and key contributions

Oxidation and oxide removal smooth silicon nanowires and change junction currents.

Forward/leakage improvements are linked to reduced roughness and defects.

Open challenges

Photocurrent decreases after smoothing; electrical improvement is not universal photodetection improvement.

Comparison context

Match diameter, absorbing volume, illumination, junction area and bias.
