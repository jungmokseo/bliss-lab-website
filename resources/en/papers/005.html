Si/Si1-xGex/Si Heterojunction PIN Nanowires Fabricated by Using an Aqueous Electroless Etching Method

Hyonik Lee, Min Su Kim, Jungmok Seo, Ja Hoon Koo, Edward Namkyu Cho, Ilgu Yun, Taeyoon Lee*

Journal of the Korean Physical Society, 59 (2), 501-504 (2011)

DOI: 10.3938/jkps.59.501 ↗

Background

A water-based etching process turns silicon/silicon–germanium layers into vertical nanowire photodiodes. The study compares their ability to distinguish light from dark with that of bulk structures.

What the study found

Aqueous etching turns silicon/silicon–germanium/silicon stacks into vertical PIN nanowire photodiodes, which the study compares with bulk structures.

The nanowire structure improves the distinction between current under illumination and current in the dark.

Questions and limits

The abstract and conclusion give different orders of dark-current reduction, so no reduction factor is quoted here. Infrared use is a proposed application rather than a demonstrated result.

How to compare the results

Compare germanium composition, junction and contact structure, illumination spectrum, and applied voltage.
