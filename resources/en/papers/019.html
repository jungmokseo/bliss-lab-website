Research overview and key contributions

Galvanic deposition places Pd nanoparticles on graphene; a PMMA overlayer selectively regulates hydrogen access.

The abstract reports hydrogen sensing with suppressed responses to the tested methane, carbon monoxide and nitrogen dioxide.

Open challenges

Editorial question: do selectivity and recovery persist under humidity, mixed gases and PMMA aging?
