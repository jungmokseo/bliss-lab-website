Evolution and clinical translation of drug delivery nanomaterials

Shabir Hassan, Gyan Prakash, Ayça Bal Öztürk, Saghi Saghazadeh, Mohammad Farhan Sohail, Jungmok Seo, Mehmet R. Dokmeci, Yu Shrike Zhang*, Ali Khademhosseini*

Nano Today, 15, 91-106 (2017)

Jungmok Seo, who now leads the BLISS Lab in the School of Electrical and Electronic Engineering at Yonsei University, is a co-author of this paper.

DOI: 10.1016/j.nantod.2017.06.008 ↗

The question behind this study

This review traces the development of drug-delivery nanomaterials and the barriers to clinical translation. It connects carrier design for particular tissues and payloads with questions of biodistribution, degradation and toxicity.

What the study shows

The review traces the development of drug-delivery nanomaterials and their clinical and approval status at the time of publication.

It connects carrier design with target tissues, diseases, drug payloads and barriers to clinical use.
