Preparation of a self-assembled organosilane coating on carbon black as a catalyst support in polymer electrolyte membrane fuel cells

Woonghee Lee, Jungmok Seo, Taeyoon Lee*, Hansung Kim*

Journal of Power Sources, 274, 1140-1146 (2015)

DOI: 10.1016/j.jpowsour.2014.10.137 ↗

Background

A water-repellent organosilane coating is applied to carbon-black catalyst supports to address corrosion in fuel cells. Cell measurements and carbon-dioxide evolution tests examine mass transport and corrosion resistance.

What the study found

A water-repellent DTS coating on carbon black is tested as a way to limit water contact and corrosion in platinum catalyst supports.

The reported tests link improved mass transfer and corrosion resistance, without platinum poisoning, to carbon-dioxide evolution and fuel-cell measurements.

Questions and limits

A question for further study is whether the accelerated-test benefits persist through start–stop cycling and prolonged operation.

How to compare the results

Compare platinum loading and active area, humidity, potential-hold conditions and gas flow, while separating the causes of voltage loss.
