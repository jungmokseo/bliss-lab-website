Research overview and key contributions

PMMA-derived few-layer graphene protects Ag nanoparticles, evaluated through oxidation and R6G Raman signals after air annealing.

The solid carbon source limits aggregation while the thin coating preserves SERS activity.

Open challenges

Editorial question: does thermal oxidation resistance translate to complex samples and long storage?

Comparison context

Match particle/coating geometry, annealing history, analyte concentration and Raman optics.

Related external research
