Counterbalanced Effect of Surface Trap and Auger Recombination on the Transverse Terahertz Carrier Dynamics in Silicon Nanowires

Chilhun In, Jungmok Seo, Hyukho Kwon, Jeongmook Choi, Sangwan Sim, Jaeseok Kim, Taeyong Kim, Taeyoon Lee, Hyunyong Choi*

IEEE Transactions on Terahertz Science and Technology, 5 (4), 605-612 (2015)

DOI: 10.1109/tthz.2015.2428619 ↗

Research context and placement

The public PDF supports surface-trap/Auger carrier dynamics in Si nanowires. This is device-physics groundwork, not a component-assembly demonstration.

Boundary case: assigned to the closest interface for navigation. Read the rationale and cross-links together; the primary theme does not delimit the study. Placement of foundational work does not establish direct bioelectronic application.

Research overview and key contributions

Optical-pump/THz-probe measurements examine competing surface-trap and Auger pathways in transverse Si-nanowire carrier decay.

Bundled, individual and encapsulated wires connect pump-fluence-dependent recombination to surface condition.

Open challenges

Editorial question: are fitted trap/Auger parameters identifiable across other geometries?

Comparison context

Match axial/transverse geometry, pump fluence, diameter/array/encapsulation, temporal resolution and initial carrier density.
