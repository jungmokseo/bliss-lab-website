A Review on Microfluidics-Based Impedance Biosensors

Yu-Shih Chen†, Chun-Hao Huang, Ping-Ching Pai, Jungmok Seo*, Kin Fong Lei*

Biosensors, 13(1), 83 (2023)

DOI: 10.3390/bios13010083 ↗

Research context and placement

Substrate, electrode and channel fabrication/joining motivate a provisional manufacturing placement. Most systems are in vitro microfluidics rather than soft interconnect–component interfaces, so ambiguity is retained.

Boundary case: assigned to the closest interface for navigation. Read the rationale and cross-links together; the primary theme does not delimit the study. Placement of foundational work does not establish direct bioelectronic application.

Research overview and key contributions

The review organizes microfluidic impedance sensors by silicon, PCB, polymer, glass, paper and stretchable substrates.

It connects flow focusing, electrode geometry and fabrication with sensitivity and miniaturization choices.

Open challenges

It supplies neither new clinical data nor a controlled ranking. Usability and commercialization claims require device-specific validation.

Comparison context

Distinguish stationary interfacial impedance from flowing-cell measurements; match frequency, electrode gap, flow and target size.
