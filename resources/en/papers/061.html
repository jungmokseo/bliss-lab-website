Rational engineering and applications of functional bioadhesives in biomedical engineering

Jae Park†, Yeonju Kim, Beomsoo Chun, Jungmok Seo*

Biotechnology Journal, 16(12), 2100231 (2021)

DOI: 10.1002/biot.202100231 ↗

Research context and placement

The abstract explicitly links bioadhesives to tissue adhesion, regeneration and sensing.

Research overview and key contributions

This review extends bioadhesives beyond bonding toward antimicrobial, cell-infiltrative, responsive, conductive and self-healing functions.

It considers adhesion retention together with regeneration and biosignal sensing.
